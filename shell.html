How to use thisTwo-page brief
What does what? A three-minute walkthrough

Purpose: help managers choose a specific, evidence-backed next step, know their proposed authority and escalate the right decision. This is a historical prototype, not live dispatch.

1. Choose where to look

O / S: separate managed service from client-operated fleets. Priority queue: ranks provisional site groups by late-leg count. Filters show how results depend on the chosen review threshold.

2. Understand the evidence

Recommendation: shows a suggested check and material counterevidence. Trip evidence: open an Event ID to inspect timestamps, quality flags and the original spreadsheet row. Lateness is against plan, not a verified SLA.

3. Prepare a decision

Who can act: explains proposed roles and escalation triggers. Prepare decision brief: assembles facts, suggestion, authority limits, owner/due and outcome. It grants no authority and sends no message.

4. Follow through

Save action: stores the record in this browser only. Closed: requires outcome evidence. Export / restore: moves your own notes between browsers; there is no shared action database.

Try IND-Hyderabad: inspect 03:00 departures and the arrival countercheck; prepare a brief before choosing an intervention. Then compare Northwind and Kestrel AQUILA. The two-page PDF contains the eight standards, build choices and pilot measures.

Your access session lasts one hour. Expiry or a failed access check locks the screen and preserves saved notes; unsaved drafts may be lost. Keep the code private. Save a backup if needed before signing out: sign-out clears this demo's local actions and drafts from this browser. Downloaded copies remain on your device. Anyone with the code can view the data.
